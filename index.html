About

Started playing basketball in 2011 with a small team in his hometown, and grew to love and perfect the game from there. In 2014 he moved to a more competitive team to play tougher games at junior-championship level, then in 2016 moved to the best team in town to keep improving. In 2019 he stepped up to senior level, working alongside foreign players and coaches — American, Serbian, Spanish, Lithuanian — and encountered different philosophies on the game of basketball along the way. He loves a challenge and doesn't back down from work.
